About GitHub & Ownership

Your website is built and published through a GitHub repository created under your own account.

You retain access to the repository, the source code, and the published website.

In practice :

  • The website remains online even if you choose not to renew the support service.
  • You are free to ask another designer to maintain or update the website in the future.
  • Your custom domain remains registered, managed, and paid for by you.

FAQ

Q. Can another designer maintain or update the code?

Yes. The website belongs to you, so you are free to work with any designer you choose. However, once the original code has been modified by a third party, I may no longer be able to provide support under the original terms.

In this context, “maintaining or updating the code” refers only to adding or replacing images, or making small text or caption edits. It does not include creating new pages, adding new features, or making major structural changes to the site.

Q. How many people are included in the “small group”?

A. At the moment, I’m planning for around 10 people. This number may increase or decrease depending on my schedule and workload. Thank you for your understanding.

Q. Do you provide a receipt or invoice?

A. Yes. All payments are declared in accordance with French law, and a receipt or invoice can be issued upon request.

Q. Who owns the website?

You do. Both the GitHub account and the code are under your name.

Q. Is the 1 year renewal mandatory? And when is it?

No, renewal is optional. Even without renewal, your website remains online. I will notify you one month before your support period ends.

Q. What is included in the free updates?

Up to two small updates per week (image additions, replacements, caption fixes, etc.).

Q. Is it possible that Natsuki stops offering this service in the future?

Yes, it is possible. My main profession is makeup artistry, and there may be a time when I become too busy to continue. If I decide to discontinue the service, I will notify you at least 6 months in advance.

Q. What if I’ve never used GitHub?

No problem. We will go through the necessary steps together when creating your account. I will handle all the complex parts and publishing setup. You do not need to touch any code.

Q. What if I want to change the structure after the site is finished?

Small adjustments are possible. Larger structural changes (new categories, major layout redesigns) will be discussed as new work.

Q. Can you create a second site for me?

It may be possible depending on my schedule. Please contact me first.

Q. Are the usage rights for my photos okay?

I assume the client has permission to use the photos. Responsibility for copyright lies with the client (you), not the designer.

Q. Is the site optimized for SEO?

Advanced SEO is not necessary for artist portfolios, but the essential metadata and structure are properly set up. This website is not meant to “rank widely,” but to reach the specific people you want to show your work to.

Q. Can the service change in the future?

Yes. To maintain and improve the quality of support, the service contents, policies, and applicable fees may be reviewed and adjusted. In such cases, any changes will be explained in advance to clients who are currently using the service, and will only be applied after receiving their understanding and approval.
